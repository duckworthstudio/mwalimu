The teacher behind the desk

A little bit about my journey as an elementary and ECD teacher, my classroom, and what I care about most: helping every child thrive.

My Teaching Philosophy

I believe learning sticks when it's hands-on, a little messy, and connected to real life. As an elementary teacher with a background in early childhood development, my classroom runs on routines, read-alouds, and a lot of encouragement — every child leaves my room believing they're capable of hard things.

Outside of teaching, you'll find me hiking with my dog, trying new recipes, or reorganizing my classroom library for the hundredth time.
